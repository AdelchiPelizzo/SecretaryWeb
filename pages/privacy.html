# Privacy Policy **Last updated: November 18, 2026** Secretary ("we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered virtual secretary service that helps businesses manage customer calls, appointments, and related business information. ## Information We Collect Secretary may collect information provided by business users, including business name, contact information, business settings, appointment settings, and information needed to provide the service. When a business user connects Google Calendar, Secretary may access Google Calendar information that the user authorizes, including calendar information and events necessary to check availability and schedule appointments. Secretary may also process information provided by callers, including telephone audio, speech transcripts, appointment requests, and information exchanged during a telephone conversation. ## How We Use Google Data Google Calendar data is used only to provide the calendar functionality requested by the business user, including: - Displaying and selecting the user's available calendars. - Checking calendar availability before scheduling appointments. - Identifying scheduling conflicts. - Creating appointments in the calendar selected by the user. Secretary processes calendar event information on its backend to determine whether a requested appointment time is available. Where an appointment time is unavailable, Secretary may send limited information derived from the availability check, such as the requested appointment details and alternative available dates and times, to its AI service provider to generate a natural-language response for the caller. Secretary does not use Google user data to develop, train, or improve generalized or foundation artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Google user data, including data derived from it, is not sold, used for advertising, or used for purposes unrelated to providing the requested calendar functionality. ## Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Secretary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. In particular: - Google user data is used only to provide or improve user-facing features that are visible and relevant within Secretary. - Google user data is not used to train or improve generalized or foundation AI or machine-learning models. - Google user data is not transferred to third parties except where necessary to provide or improve the requested user-facing functionality, with the user's consent, or where required by law. - Human access to Google user data is restricted to circumstances permitted by the Google API Services User Data Policy, including where necessary for security, legal compliance, or user-requested support. ## Artificial Intelligence and Third-Party AI Providers Secretary uses OpenAI services through the OpenAI API to provide AI-powered telephone secretary functionality. Depending on the feature being used, information processed by OpenAI may include: - Caller audio submitted for speech transcription. - Speech transcripts and conversation context needed to understand and respond to callers. - Business configuration and instructions needed to provide the secretary service. - Appointment details needed to generate responses. - Limited calendar availability information, such as requested appointment times and alternative available time slots. Secretary sends only information considered necessary for the relevant AI-powered feature. Google Calendar event information is processed by Secretary's backend for availability checks. Secretary does not intentionally send complete Google Calendar event records, descriptions, or attendee information to OpenAI when generating alternative appointment suggestions. OpenAI processes information submitted through its API to provide the requested AI functionality, subject to the applicable OpenAI API terms and data-processing arrangements. Google user data is not authorized for use in training or improving OpenAI's generalized or foundation models. Secretary does not authorize AI providers to use Google user data for unrelated purposes. ## Storage and Security Information, including authorized Google Calendar connection data, is stored using appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect it against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. OAuth credentials and tokens are used only to maintain the authorized connection between Secretary and the user's Google Calendar. Access to stored information is restricted to what is necessary to operate and maintain the service. ## User Control Users may disconnect their Google Calendar from Secretary at any time. Users may also revoke Secretary's access through their Google Account security settings. Disconnecting Google Calendar prevents Secretary from using that connection to access the user's calendar. ## Data Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the user's account, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Information submitted to third-party service providers may be subject to the retention periods and data-processing terms applicable to those providers. When a user disconnects Google Calendar, Secretary will no longer use that connection to access Google Calendar. ## Third-Party Service Providers Secretary uses third-party service providers to operate the service. These may include: - **OpenAI:** AI-powered speech transcription and conversational response generation. - **Hosting and infrastructure providers:** Hosting and operating the Secretary application. - **Database providers:** Storing business, appointment, and service information. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and subject to applicable contractual and data-protection requirements. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
