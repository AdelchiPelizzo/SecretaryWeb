# Privacy Policy **Last updated: September 2026** Secretary ("we", "us", or "our") provides an AI-powered virtual secretary service that helps businesses manage customer calls, appointments, and related business information. ## Information We Collect Secretary may collect information provided by business users, including business name, contact information, business settings, appointment settings, and information needed to provide the service. When a business user connects Google Calendar, Secretary may access Google Calendar information that the user authorizes, including calendar information and events necessary to check availability and schedule appointments. ## How We Use Google Data Google Calendar data is used only to provide the calendar functionality requested by the business user, including checking calendar availability and creating or managing appointments. We do not sell Google user data. We do not use Google user data for advertising or unrelated purposes. We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except where necessary to provide the requested service or when required by law. ## Storage and Security Information, including authorized Google Calendar connection data, is stored using appropriate technical and organizational measures designed to protect it against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. OAuth credentials and tokens are used only to maintain the authorized connection between Secretary and the user's Google Calendar. ## User Control Users may disconnect their Google Calendar from Secretary at any time. Users may also revoke Secretary's access through their Google Account security settings. ## Data Retention We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain the user's account, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. When a user disconnects Google Calendar, Secretary will no longer use that connection to access Google Calendar. ## Third Parties Secretary uses service providers such as hosting, database, and cloud service providers to operate the service. These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services. ## Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new update date. ## Contact For questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, contact: **[adelchi@adelforce.com](mailto:adelchi@adelforce.com)**
